Delete Your Account

Sad to see you go! Here's how to remove your One Tap Tower account and everything in it.

One Tap Tower · Developer: HoloCrux · Requests: kavinvigneshplaystore@gmail.com

Fastest: delete it in the game

If the game is still on your phone, you can delete your account yourself in a few seconds. You'll need an internet connection.

  1. Open One Tap Tower and stay on the home screen
  2. Tap the gear in the top-right corner
  3. Scroll down to Account and tap Delete
  4. Confirm twice — your account is gone

The Account section only appears when Settings is opened from the home screen, not from the pause menu during a run.

What is kept

  • Purchase records. Google Play keeps its own records of in-app purchases for tax and accounting. We can't delete those, and anything you bought is not restored once the account is gone.
  • Your deletion e-mail and our reply, for up to 12 months, as proof that your request was handled.
  • Limited security logs kept by our service providers (Unity, Google) for the periods set in their own policies.

How long it takes

Deleting in the game takes effect straight away. E-mail requests are completed within 30 days, and we'll reply to confirm. If we can't find your account from the details you gave, we'll write back to ask for a little more.

Deletion is permanent — your coins, characters and progress can't be brought back afterwards. Want to know more about what we keep? Read our Privacy Policy.
