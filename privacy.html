The short version

One Tap Tower never asks for your real name, e-mail address, contacts, photos or location. You play under a nickname you choose. Your progress lives on your phone and, when you are online, is backed up to your game account so you can keep it. You can delete that account at any time from inside the game.

This policy explains how HoloCrux ("we", "us") handles information when you play One Tap Tower (the "game") on Android, and when you visit this website. If you have a question that this page doesn't answer, e-mail us — a real person reads every message.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Nickname. The name you pick when you first play. It is shown on leaderboards and to your friends. Please don't use your real full name.
  • Friends. The friend codes you add, the friend requests you send or accept, and the friends you remove.
  • Messages to us. If you e-mail us (for example a support question or a deletion request), we receive your e-mail address and whatever you write.

Information created as you play

  • Game progress. Scores, best height, coins, tokens, level, characters and skins you own, power-up upgrades, missions, awards, daily streak and Lucky Spin state, and your settings (sound, music, vibration).
  • Game account identifier. A random player ID created by Unity Authentication the first time the game goes online. It is not linked to your name, e-mail address or phone number.
  • Country. A two-letter country code read from your SIM card or mobile network (falling back to your phone's region setting), used only to place you on your country's leaderboard. We never request location permission and never use GPS.
  • Google Play Games link (optional). If you choose to back up your progress with Google Play Games, your game account is linked to your Play Games player ID. We do not receive your Google account e-mail address or password.

Information collected automatically by our service providers

  • Device and connection data such as device model, operating-system version, language, IP address and app version, which online services need to work and to protect against abuse.
  • Advertising data when you choose to watch a rewarded ad — see Ads below.
  • Purchase records when you buy something — see Purchases below.

How we use information

  • To run the game: keep your progress, show leaderboards, and let you add and climb with friends.
  • To back up and restore your progress (including onto a new phone with Google Play Games).
  • To deliver rewarded ads you choose to watch, and the rewards that come with them.
  • To process and restore in-app purchases.
  • To keep the game fair, secure and working, and to fix bugs.
  • To answer your messages and deletion requests.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it to build profiles about you outside the game.

Ads

One Tap Tower only shows rewarded ads — the ones you choose to watch for a second chance, double coins or a Lucky Spin. There are no pop-up ads between runs. When an ad is shown, the ad network may use your device's advertising ID and basic device information to choose an ad, measure it and prevent fraud, and may show personalised ads where the law allows.

You can reset your advertising ID, or delete it to opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings under Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads (or Settings ▸ Google ▸ Ads on some phones). Where the law requires consent for personalised ads (for example in the EEA or the UK), ads are shown only on the basis the law allows.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or bank details. We receive a purchase receipt (order ID, product and time) so the game can deliver and restore what you bought. Google keeps its own records of your purchases, as required for tax and accounting, and those are not removed when you delete your game account.

What other players can see

  • Your nickname, best score and country on the leaderboards.
  • Your friend code (your nickname plus a short number), if you share it.
  • Your nickname and score to the friends you add. You can remove any friend at any time from the Friends screen, and cancel any friend request you've sent.

There is no chat in the game, and no player can see your private details.

How long we keep information

  • Your progress is kept on your phone until you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Your online game account (cloud backup, scores, friends) is kept while you play, and is deleted when you ask us to delete it.
  • Deletion e-mails and our replies are kept for up to 12 months, as a record that your request was handled.
  • Our service providers may keep limited logs for security and accounting for the periods set out in their own policies.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your game account at any time, right inside the game: open Settings from the home screen, scroll down to Account, tap Delete Account and confirm. This removes your scores from the leaderboards, your friends, your cloud backup and your game account, and resets the game on your phone.

If you no longer have the game installed, you can request deletion on this website or e-mail kavinvigneshplaystore@gmail.com. We complete requests within 30 days.

Children

One Tap Tower is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Information sent between the game and our services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to the game's back end is limited to the developer, and administrative keys are stored in a protected secret store, never inside the app. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell you if a breach affects you where the law requires.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe, the UK GDPR, India's DPDP Act or US state laws such as the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict some processing, and to withdraw consent. To use any of these rights, e-mail us. We will answer within 30 days and will not treat you differently for asking. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Where the GDPR applies, we process information to provide the game you asked for (contract), for our legitimate interests in keeping it secure and working, to comply with the law, and — for personalised ads — with your consent. Our service providers may process data outside your country; they use recognised safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking of its own. GitHub and Google may log your IP address as part of delivering the pages. The deletion request form does not send anything by itself — it opens your own e-mail app with a pre-filled message that you choose to send.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page, and for important changes we will let you know in the game.
